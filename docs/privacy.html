Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the mobile game Leap of Void (the "App").

The short version

Leap of Void collects no data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no tracking of any kind.

Data collection

The App does not collect, transmit, or share any personal information or usage data. It does not require an internet connection to play.

Local storage

The App stores a small amount of data on your device only, so the game works as expected:

  • Your best score
  • Your settings (sound, music, and haptics toggles)

This data never leaves your device. Deleting the App deletes it.

Third parties

The App contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or social SDKs.

Children

The App does not collect data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future version of the App changes any of the above (for example, if ads are added), this policy will be updated before that version is released.
